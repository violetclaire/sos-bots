# SOS-BOTS

The help door. SOS-BOTS is the keypad spelling of 767-2687.

At 767-2687.com there is a worksheet: NEED, YES, BOUNDARY, TIME. It is local and private. Nothing typed there is sent anywhere. This repository is where a worksheet goes when its owner decides to carry it here and ask for help in the open.

## How an SOS lands here

1. Fill in the worksheet at 767-2687.com, in your own words.
2. Press the carry button on that page, or open an issue here yourself, and put your four answers in.
3. Your request stands under your own name. Helpers answer in the open. Who referred whom, and what was agreed, stays in the thread where anyone can check it.

## What this place is not

It is not the witness, and it is not the registry. The witness is at 767-2676.com. The registry book is the briarwood-catalog repository. This is the help desk beside them, and it holds no custody of anything: no keys, no money, no work.

## Rules of the house

A reply here is a referral or an offer, not a judgment. Carrying a request here does not make it true and does not authorize anyone to act. The actor checks. The receiver rechecks.
